

# Backup and Recovery

## Objective
Recover AD after mistakes or failures.

---

## Tasks

- System State Backup
- Restore deleted users
- Enable AD Recycle Bin
- Backup DNS
- Test restore process

---

## Why Important
If Domain Controller fails, business operations can stop.

---

## Best Practice
Do regular backups and test recovery.

---

## What I Learned
Backups are useless unless recovery is tested.
