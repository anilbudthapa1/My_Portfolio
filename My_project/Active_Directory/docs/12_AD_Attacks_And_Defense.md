

# AD Attacks and Defense

## Objective
Understand attacker techniques to defend better.

---

## Common Attacks

## Kerberoasting
Steal service ticket hashes.

## Pass the Hash
Reuse password hashes.

## Golden Ticket
Forge Kerberos tickets.

## LLMNR Poisoning
Capture credentials on local network.

## Privilege Escalation
Gain higher access.

---

## Defenses

- Strong passwords
- MFA
- Disable old protocols
- Monitor logs
- Patch systems
- Least privilege

---

## Important Note
Use only in your own lab.
